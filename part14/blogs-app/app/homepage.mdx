# Blog App

Welcome to **Blog App** - a place to write, share, and read short posts built as part of the [Full Stack Open](https://fullstackopen.com/en/) course.

This homepage is written in Markdown and rendered through MDX, so it can mix plain text with JSX components whenever needed.

## What you can do here

- Create an account and log in
- Write and publish your own blog posts
- Browse posts from other users
- Keep a personal reading list
- Mark posts as read once you've gone through them

## Getting started

If you already have an account, head over to the [login page](/login) to sign in. New here? You can [register](/register) in just a few seconds.

Once logged in, visit your [personal page](/me) to generate an API token and check your reading list.

---

Built with Next.js, Drizzle ORM, and NextAuth.js.
